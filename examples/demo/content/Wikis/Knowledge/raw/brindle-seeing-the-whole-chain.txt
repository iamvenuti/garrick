Seeing the Whole Chain: Control Towers for Mid-Sized Manufacturers
White paper. June 2026. Downloaded from the Brindle Systems website, 14 September 2026.

Mid-sized manufacturers run on spreadsheets and phone calls. When a supplier slips, the first sign is often a production line waiting for a part.

A control tower brings order status, carrier tracking and supplier commitments into one view. Brindle Beacon, our control tower for manufacturers with between fifty and five hundred suppliers, connects to the ERP in weeks, not months.

Results our customers report

- Late inbound shipments down by 30 percent in the first year.
- Beacon pays for itself within nine months.
- Planners spend half as much time chasing suppliers.

Where to start

Start with the parts that stop the line. Map which of them come from a single supplier, and watch those first. Visibility is worth most where there is no second source.
