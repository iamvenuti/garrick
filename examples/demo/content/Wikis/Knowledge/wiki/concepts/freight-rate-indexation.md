# Freight rate indexation

Linking a freight rate, or its fuel element, to a published index that is reviewed on a schedule, in place of a fixed surcharge.

## What sources say

- About half of new European road contracts in 2026 carried an index-linked fuel clause, up from a quarter two years earlier; accept one only when it works both ways and names a public index. [[../sources/pellow-strand-freight-rate-outlook-autumn-2026|Freight Rate Outlook, Autumn 2026]]
- Carriers usually offer an index clause together with a volume commitment, so Pellow & Strand Research, quoted by The Freight Ledger, advise negotiating the two as one package: see [[../concepts/volume-commitments|volume commitments]]. [[../sources/freight-ledger-shippers-push-back-on-volume-commitments|Shippers push back on volume commitments]]
