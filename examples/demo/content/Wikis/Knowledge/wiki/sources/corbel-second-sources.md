# Second Sources

A practice guide from the Corbel Supply Institute, based on a survey of 210 manufacturers in seven European countries and interviews with 18 purchasing managers.

- Sixty-two percent of the manufacturers surveyed had at least one single-source part that would stop a line within a month.
- Qualifying a second source for a machined, cast or moulded part took a median of eleven weeks. Rubber and polymer parts took longest, because of ageing and pressure tests.
- The steps: drawings and the material specification, sample tests, a first-article inspection, then a trial order.
- Hold bridge stock while the second source is approved, sized to the qualification time.
- An 80/20 split keeps a second supplier; a token order once a year usually loses it.
