THE CHAIR

The Kestle is built on one curved steel frame, with no joints to work loose. Its mesh back gives where your back does, and the seat tilts with you, not against you. Ines Calder, who designed it, sat in forty prototypes before she signed off this one. It comes in graphite, sand, moss and white, and every Kestle carries our ten-year warranty.

It is made to be the one chair an office buys for every desk: easy to set up, easy to live with, and still sound when the lease runs out.
