Birch & Co: sizing assumptions
Friday 11 September 2026, 11:00 to 11:40. Video call.
Speakers: Iris Bell, Lena Moss, Sam Rivera

Iris Bell: Sam, Lena runs finance. I asked her to look at your assumptions before Wednesday.
Lena Moss: I read the sheet. The market numbers are fine. The price isn't.
Sam Rivera: Go on.
Lena Moss: You've used our list price. We won't see list price. Price it at our export list less the distributor's margin. That's the money we'll actually see.
Sam Rivera: What margin do distributors take in the region?

Lena Moss: Use thirty. If it works at thirty, it works.
Sam Rivera: That takes Birch's revenue down by nearly a third against the sheet. The share assumptions stay as they are.
Iris Bell: Which scenario goes in the memo?
Sam Rivera: The middle one, three and a half percent by year two. Low is two, high is five.
Lena Moss: Show the low one too. The board will ask what happens if it goes badly.
Sam Rivera: I'll show all three, with the middle one carried. Can I have the export price list?
