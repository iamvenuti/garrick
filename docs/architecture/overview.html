Garrick: a shell around Claude Code or Codex

Navigate, then work

The Garrick App is the control panel over your zones, projects and threads: where each stands, what is due, park or wake a thread, tick an action.

It opens a thread where you work: the Claude or Codex desktop app, or cmux with a terminal tab per thread. Say “hold this” when interrupted and “back” when you return.

The record

Each thread has one resume point: what is done, the next action, and what was only in your head.

Meetings keeps every conversation, Knowledge what you read. All of it is plain Markdown in git, on your Mac. Mail, files and recordings are filed into it for you.

Underneath, and the guard

Claude or Codex is the model doing the work across the whole record, whichever app you use it in, and it reads the rules first.

The rules and the walls you declare apply to everything in the record. A check before every commit catches links, names and copied wording across a wall, within limits: see Principles and limits.
