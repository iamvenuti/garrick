# Market sizing

Working out what a market is worth to one seller: the whole market first, then the part its product and price can reach, then a realistic share of that part.

## What sources say

- The Nordic Fluid Equipment Council splits mid-range pump sales by application, which lets a seller cut the market down to its own segment. Its figures come from member reports and are not audited. Brands entering since 2020 held a median two percent of their segment after two years, the best quarter five percent or more.
