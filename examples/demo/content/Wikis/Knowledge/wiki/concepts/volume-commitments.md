# Volume commitments

A shipper's promise to send a minimum volume, given in return for a lower or held rate. What it costs depends on how a shortfall is counted and charged.

## What sources say

- Shortfall clauses usually charge unshipped pallets at the full rate, quarter by quarter, so a commitment set near the busiest quarter costs money in the slowest. Pellow & Strand Research, quoted, advise committing to the slowest quarter's volume and negotiating the commitment together with any fuel index clause: see [[../concepts/freight-rate-indexation|freight rate indexation]].
