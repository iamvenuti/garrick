# Distributor-led entry

Entering a new country through an independent distributor that buys, stocks and resells the product, instead of setting up a sales company there.

## What sources say

- In the Nordic mid-range pump market, independent distributors carry 71 percent of sales by value, on the Nordic Fluid Equipment Council's member figures. [[../sources/nfec-nordic-pump-market-review-2026|Nordic Pump Market Review 2026]]
- Varne & Lisle say exporters entering the Nordics most often appoint one distributor, and that the agreement decides how well it works: see [[../concepts/distribution-agreements|distribution agreements]]. [[../sources/varne-lisle-distribution-agreements-in-the-nordics|Distribution Agreements in the Nordic Countries]]
