Garrick: a shell around Claude Code or Codex

Navigate, then work

The Garrick App is the control panel over your zones, projects and pieces of work: where each stands, what is due, park or wake one, tick an action.

It opens a piece of work where you work: the Claude or Codex desktop app, or cmux with a terminal tab per piece of work. Say “hold this” when interrupted and “back” when you return.

The record

It holds what you’re working on, what was said and what you read: your pieces of work, Meetings and Knowledge. Documents, mail and transcripts are filed into the part they belong to for you.

Each piece of work has one resume point: what is done, the next action, and what was only in your head. All of it is plain Markdown in git, on your Mac.

Underneath, and the guard

Claude or Codex is the model doing the work across the whole record, whichever app you use it in, and it reads the rules first.

The rules and the walls you declare apply to everything in the record. A check before every commit in each zone catches links, names and copied wording across a wall, within limits: see Principles and limits.
