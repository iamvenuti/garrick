# Freight Terms

The options brief for Acme's freight contract with Cobalt Freight, which renews on 31 October: stay, renegotiate, or split the volume.

## The ask

Dana Whitlock, at the [[Meetings/wiki/sources/260902-acme-kick-off|kick-off]]: Cobalt had hinted at "high single digits". Sam offered to be in the room when they put a number on it, and to brief the options before the renewal.

## State of play

### Resume here

**Where it stands, 25 September 2026.** The brief is drafted with three options: renew at three percent against a commitment of twelve hundred pallets a quarter, renew at nine percent with no commitment, or split the volume with a second carrier. Cobalt's written offer has not arrived, and Dana wants the brief by Friday 2 October whether it has or not.
