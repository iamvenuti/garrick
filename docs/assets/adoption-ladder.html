Start small. Climb when you want to.

Most people start here

Level 1

Organise

You add

Claude or Codex in its desktop app, and the Garrick folders.

You gain

A resume point for every piece of work. Meetings and knowledge kept as second brains locally. Walls between the parties you declare.

Level 2

Feed it

You add

Inbox folders, mail alias ingestion, recorder transcripts.

You gain

Conversations and mail filed for you, not pasted by hand.

Level 3

Own the interface

You add

Obsidian to browse, and Cmux for parallel sessions.

You gain

Your work no longer lives inside one vendor's app.

Level 4

Work from anywhere

You add

The assistants' remote and voice features.

You gain

Your workspace answers from your phone, by voice.

Each level works on its own. Most people never need all four.
