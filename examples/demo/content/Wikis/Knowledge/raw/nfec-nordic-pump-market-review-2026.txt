Nordic Fluid Equipment Council
Nordic Pump Market Review 2026
Annual statistics. Published June 2026.
Downloaded from the Council's public statistics page, 8 September 2026.

Summary

Sales of industrial pumps in Denmark, Finland, Iceland, Norway and Sweden reached 1.12 billion euros in 2025, up 2.4 percent on 2024. Volumes were flat; prices did the rest.

Mid-range pumps

Pumps listed at between 2,000 and 12,000 euros a unit sold 214 million euros in 2025. By application:

- Building services: 98 million euros
- Municipal water: 48 million euros
- Light industry: 41 million euros
- Process industry: 27 million euros

Channels

Independent distributors carried 71 percent of mid-range sales by value. Manufacturers' own sales companies carried the rest.

New entrants

Eleven brands entered the Nordic mid-range between 2020 and 2024. Two years after entry, the median held two percent of its application segment; the best quarter held five percent or more.

Methodology: compiled from sales reported by the Council's 46 member companies, and from national import statistics. Member data is not audited.
