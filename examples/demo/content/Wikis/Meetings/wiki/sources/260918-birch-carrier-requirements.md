# Birch carrier requirements

Call, 18 September 2026. Sam Rivera with Arno Vale and Iris Bell.

## Decided

- What Birch needs from a carrier: two departures a week at least, door to door with a tail lift, and claims handled properly.
- Sam puts the requirements on one page and takes them to Cobalt Freight and at least one other carrier.

## Wording that matters

- Arno: "Two departures a week at least. The distributor won't hold more than ten days of stock, so one a week leaves them short."
- Arno: "I'd rather pay a little more per pallet than spend a month chasing a damaged one."
- Volumes: "Forty to sixty pallets a month to start."
