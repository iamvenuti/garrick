# Cobalt Freight renewal, for Acme

Video call, 15 September 2026. Sam Rivera and Dana Whitlock (Acme) with Marta Quill (Cobalt Freight).

## Decided

- Nothing agreed. Acme will look at Cobalt's offer properly once it is in writing.

## Wording that matters

- Marta's opening position: "We're proposing nine percent on the renewal."
- Her offer: "If Acme commits to twelve hundred pallets a quarter, I can hold it at three percent." Dana: twelve hundred is above last year's volume; Marta: "close to your peak quarter."
- Cobalt wants a fuel index clause, reviewed quarterly, in place of the fixed surcharge. Marta confirmed it moves "both ways."
- Marta, unprompted: "Between us. The northern lane is full until February. I wouldn't promise anyone new space up there before then." Dana: it "doesn't change much for us."
