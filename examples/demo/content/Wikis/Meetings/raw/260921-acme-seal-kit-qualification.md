# Acme Corp: seal-kit qualification

Monday 21 September 2026, 14:00. Video call.
Present: Felix Rook, Owen Pike, Sam Rivera

**Sam Rivera:** Felix, before Thursday's review I'd like to know what it takes to approve a second seal-kit maker.

**Felix Rook:** Drawings and the material spec go out first. They send samples. The samples go on the test rig for eight weeks: pressure, temperature, the lot. Then a first-article inspection on their first real batch.

**Sam Rivera:** So from samples to approval?

**Felix Rook:** Ten weeks, if nothing fails. If a sample fails, add a month.

**Owen Pike:** And the maker has to want our volumes. Some won't bother below a few thousand kits a year.

**Felix Rook:** Meanwhile Keld stays the only approved source. I can't let an unapproved kit on the line, whatever the price.

**Sam Rivera:** If Dana chooses seal kits first on Thursday, when would samples need to arrive to have a second source by spring?

**Felix Rook:** By the middle of October. Later than that and you're into April.

**Sam Rivera:** Understood. Can you send me your checklist, so the map can show the steps?

**Felix Rook:** I'll send it by Wednesday.
