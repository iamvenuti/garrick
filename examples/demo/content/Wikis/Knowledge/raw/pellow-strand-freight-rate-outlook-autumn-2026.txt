Freight Rate Outlook, Autumn 2026
Analyst note. Published 1 September 2026.
Saved from the Pellow & Strand client portal, 7 September 2026.

Summary

Contract rates on European road freight lanes are set to rise between four and seven percent at renewals falling due between now and the end of the first quarter of 2027. Driver wages and tolls account for most of the increase; fuel is a smaller and more volatile share.

Indexation

Carriers are replacing fixed fuel surcharges with index-linked clauses reviewed quarterly. In the contracts we reviewed this year, a little over half of new agreements carried such a clause, against about a quarter two years ago. Where the clause works in both directions, shippers have generally accepted it. Where it only moves upward, we advise against signing.

Capacity

Northbound capacity into the Nordic countries is tight through the first quarter of 2027. Several carriers have added cross-dock space in southern Sweden, but that space comes on line in the spring. Shippers planning new northbound volume before March should expect to pay a premium or wait.

What we advise

1. Treat any renewal increase above seven percent as a starting position, not a price.
2. Trade volume commitments for rate only against a forecast you would sign yourself.
3. Accept indexation only when it works both ways and names a public index.

Methodology: based on 140 contract renewals shared with us by shippers between January and August 2026, and interviews with eleven carriers.
