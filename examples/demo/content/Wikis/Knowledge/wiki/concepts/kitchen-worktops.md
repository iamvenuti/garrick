# Kitchen worktops

Choosing a worktop material: how it looks, what upkeep it needs, whether damage can be put right, and what it costs fitted.

## What sources say

- Homewright's six-month test: oak is the most forgiving but needs oiling, laminate is the cheapest but cannot be repaired, and composite is the hardest-wearing but costs the most, about 400 euros a metre fitted against 250 for oak. [[../sources/homewright-worktops-compared|Worktops Compared]]
