Distribution Agreements in the Nordic Countries: A Briefing for Exporters
Client briefing. Published 12 May 2026. Downloaded from the Varne & Lisle website, 23 September 2026.

This briefing is general information, not legal advice on any particular agreement.

Exporters entering the Nordic countries most often appoint one distributor, which buys, stocks and resells their products. The agreement decides how well that works.

Exclusivity. Distributors commonly ask for exclusivity in their territory. Grant it country by country, and tie it to minimum purchase targets that can be measured each year.

Term. Start with a fixed term of two or three years, renewable, rather than an open-ended agreement.

Ending it. Rules on notice, and in some cases on compensation when a distributor is terminated, differ between the countries. Check them for each country before you sign, not when you want to leave.

Prices, marketing and spares. Set out who sets resale prices, within what competition law allows, who pays for marketing, and what spares the distributor keeps in stock.

Varne & Lisle advises exporters on distribution and agency agreements in Denmark, Finland, Norway and Sweden.
