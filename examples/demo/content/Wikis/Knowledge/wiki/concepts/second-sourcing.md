# Second sourcing

Approving a second supplier for a part that has only one, so the line keeps running if the first one stops.

## What sources say

- The Corbel Supply Institute puts qualification at a median of eleven weeks for machined, cast or moulded parts, longer for rubber and polymer ones, and advises bridge stock while it runs: see [[../concepts/safety-stock|safety stock]]. An 80/20 split keeps the second supplier interested. [[../sources/corbel-second-sources|Second Sources]]
