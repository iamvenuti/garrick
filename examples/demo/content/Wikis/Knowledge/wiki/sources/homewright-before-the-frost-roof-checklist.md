# Before the Frost

A consumer article from Homewright, a home improvement magazine, on small roof repairs before the winter.

- Slipped tiles let water in, and the first frost widens the gap.
- Scaffolding is often half the cost of a small roof repair, whoever does the work.
- Most home insurance treats slipped and worn tiles as wear and tear, and does not pay to fix them. Storm damage usually is covered, but a claim may be refused if the roof was already in poor repair.
- Its advice: a written quote with the scaffolding priced separately, and the work done before the first hard frosts.
