# Arc chair: launch brief

Owner: Robin Hale. Written {arc_start_long}.

## What it is

The Arc is a task chair with a single curved steel frame, a mesh back and a seat that tilts with the sitter. Ines Calder designed it. It comes in four colours: graphite, sand, moss and white.

## Who it is for

Offices that want one chair for every desk, and the dealers who furnish them. Second: universities, for libraries and study rooms.

## The message

One chair, built to last ten years, that people choose to sit in. Durability first, comfort second, price last.

## Dates

Ships to dealers on {ship_long}. The launch email goes out that morning.

## The dealer offer

10 percent off a first order of 20 chairs or more, until the end of the quarter, if Nadia's margins allow it.

## What dealers get

The launch email, which they can forward; the dealer one-pager; four product photographs from Ines.
