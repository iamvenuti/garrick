# Birch Nordic entry kick-off

Call, 4 September 2026. Sam Rivera with Theo Marsh and Iris Bell.

## Decided

- The question is whether Birch enters the Nordics next year, and if so how.
- Three pieces of work, in order: market sizing (memo by 16 September), carrier choice, then a launch plan once the board has decided.
- The board meets on 28 September. Iris: what goes to it "has to be a recommendation, not a menu."

## Wording that matters

- Theo: "If the numbers don't hold up, I'd rather hear it in September than in March."
- Theo on confidentiality: "I don't want to know who else you work for, and I don't want them knowing about us." Sam gave his word that nothing from one client goes into another client's work.
