# Acme freight spend baseline

Video call, 10 September 2026. Sam Rivera with Gil Thorne; Dana Whitlock joined late.

## Decided

- Last year's freight with Cobalt Freight is the baseline for the renewal: 4,470 pallets for 920,000 euros, about 206 euros a pallet.
- Sam puts the baseline on one page for Dana before the renewal call on 15 September.

## Wording that matters

- By quarter: "Nine hundred and sixty in the first quarter, twelve hundred and ten in the second, twelve hundred and forty in the third, a thousand and sixty in the fourth."
- Lanes: "Eighty-five percent on the southern lanes."
- Fuel: "a fixed surcharge, eleven percent on top of the base rate. It hasn't moved in two years."
- Dana: "Then I want to know them better than she does before the fifteenth."
