# Acme seal-kit qualification

Video call, 21 September 2026. Sam Rivera with Felix Rook and Owen Pike.

## Decided

- Nothing decided: background for the supplier map review on 24 September.
- Keld Seals stays the only approved seal-kit source until a second maker passes qualification.

## Wording that matters

- The steps: drawings and the material spec, then samples, "on the test rig for eight weeks", then a first-article inspection on the first real batch.
- Timing: "Ten weeks, if nothing fails. If a sample fails, add a month."
- For a second source by spring, samples are needed "By the middle of October. Later than that and you're into April."
- Felix: "I can't let an unapproved kit on the line, whatever the price."
