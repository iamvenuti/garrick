# Distribution agreements

The contract between a seller and its distributor: territory and exclusivity, targets, term, how it ends, prices, marketing and spares.

## What sources say

- Varne & Lisle advise exclusivity country by country, tied to minimum purchases each year, a fixed first term of two or three years, and checking each country's notice and termination rules before signing. Their briefing calls itself general information, not legal advice. [[../sources/varne-lisle-distribution-agreements-in-the-nordics|Distribution Agreements in the Nordic Countries]]
