# Acme Corp: pump-line supplier map

Prepared for Dana Whitlock by Sam Rivera. First sent 11 September 2026.

## Method

Owen Pike's ERP export of 4 September, with the duplicate records from the old system removed. Tier one is complete for the pump line. Tier two is shown only where the export names it; the gaps are left blank on purpose.

## What breaks the line

Three parts on the pump line come from a single supplier.

| Part | Supplier | Annual spend | Stock on hand | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Seal kit | Keld Seals | 186,000 euros | two weeks | Every pump needs one; no approved alternative |
| Motor housing | Halden Castings | 410,000 euros | five weeks | Only approved source since 2021 |
| Control board | Orrin Electronics | 352,000 euros | four weeks | Firmware is tied to this board |

## Everything else

Shaft blanks, pump casings, fasteners and o-ring packs each have at least two approved sources. No action proposed.

## Recommendation

Fix one part properly before starting the next. Seal kits first: the least stock, the lowest cost to qualify a second maker.
