Carriers race for Nordic capacity
By staff reporter. Published 10 September 2026. Saved from the web, 21 September 2026.

Road carriers are adding capacity on routes into the Nordic countries after a year in which northbound space ran short.

Cobalt Freight opened a cross-dock in southern Sweden in August. In a statement, the company said the site would "add significant northbound capacity from next spring". Cobalt runs its northern service three times a week and did not comment on rates.

Fernway Logistics, which has concentrated on the German and Dutch markets, said it would start a twice-weekly northern service in January. Its commercial director said Fernway would "price below the market to win the first customers", without giving figures.

Analysts expect the new space to ease pressure by the middle of 2027. Until then, shippers with new northbound volume face a choice between paying for scarce space now and waiting for the spring.
