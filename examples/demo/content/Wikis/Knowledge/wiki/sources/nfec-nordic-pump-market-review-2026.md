# Nordic Pump Market Review 2026

Annual statistics from the Nordic Fluid Equipment Council, a trade body, compiled from the sales its 46 members report and from national import statistics. The review gives its publication month only. Member data is not audited.

- Industrial pump sales in the five Nordic countries were 1.12 billion euros in 2025, up 2.4 percent, on flat volumes.
- Mid-range pumps, listed at 2,000 to 12,000 euros a unit, sold 214 million euros. By application: building services 98 million, municipal water 48 million, light industry 41 million, process industry 27 million.
- Independent distributors carried 71 percent of mid-range sales by value.
- Of eleven brands that entered the mid-range from 2020 to 2024, the median held two percent of its segment two years after entry; the best quarter held five percent or more.
