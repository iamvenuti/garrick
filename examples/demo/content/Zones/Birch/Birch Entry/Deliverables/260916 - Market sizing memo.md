# Birch & Co: the Nordic market for mid-range pumps

Memo for Theo Marsh and Iris Bell, Sam Rivera. 16 September 2026.

## The answer

The Nordic market for mid-range industrial pumps is about forty million euros a year. A realistic share for Birch by the end of year two is three to four percent: roughly one and a half million euros a year.

## How the number was built

- Market size from public trade statistics for the five Nordic countries, cut to the price band Birch sells in.
- Share from what comparable entrants reached in two years, checked against the six distributors Iris met in June.
- Three scenarios: low (two percent), middle (three and a half), high (five). The memo uses the middle.

## What it means

Enough to justify a distributor, not enough to justify a subsidiary. Recommendation for the board: enter through one distributor, launch in the spring, and choose a carrier before Christmas.
