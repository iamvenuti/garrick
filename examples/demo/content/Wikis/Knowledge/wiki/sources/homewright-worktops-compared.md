# Worktops Compared

A consumer test from Homewright: three worktops fitted in its test kitchen and used for six months. The article gives its publication month only.

- Oak: the warmest and the most forgiving, since scratches and burns sand out. It needs oiling about twice a year and marks with standing water. About 250 euros a metre, fitted.
- Laminate: the cheapest, about 90 euros a metre fitted. Edge chips cannot be repaired.
- Composite: the hardest-wearing in Homewright's tests, with no oiling, and it resists stains. A chip needs a specialist, and it is heavy to fit. About 400 euros a metre, fitted.
