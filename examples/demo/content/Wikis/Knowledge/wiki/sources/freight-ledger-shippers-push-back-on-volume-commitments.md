# Shippers push back on volume commitments

A trade press article from The Freight Ledger on carriers trading rate holds for promised volumes at this autumn's renewals.

- Most offers carry a shortfall clause: pallets promised but not shipped are charged, usually at the full rate and counted quarter by quarter. Committing close to the busiest quarter means paying for empty space in the slowest.
- Shippers are asking for the commitment to be counted over the year, and for shortfalls at a reduced rate. Carriers accept the first more readily.
- Pellow & Strand Research, quoted by the paper: shippers should "commit to what you ship in your slowest quarter, not your best", and negotiate a commitment and a fuel index clause as one package.
