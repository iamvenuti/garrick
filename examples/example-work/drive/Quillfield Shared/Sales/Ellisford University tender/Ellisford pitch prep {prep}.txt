Leo Brandt: Quality and durability forty percent, price thirty, sustainability twenty, service ten. Which is good news for us. If we lead on price we lose to the cheap ones. Lead on how long it lasts.
Robin Hale: The ten-year warranty, then. And last year's learning centre: no frame failures in a year.
Leo Brandt: Exactly. Durability first, the warranty, the case study. Price goes on the last page, and we don't apologise for it.
Robin Hale: What about sustainability? Twenty percent is a lot to leave to a paragraph.
Leo Brandt: That's what I wanted to ask you. Can you write that page? The recycled steel, the take-back scheme, whatever we can actually stand behind.
Robin Hale: Yes. I'll write the sustainability page. Recycled steel in the frames, the take-back scheme, and the warranty, because a chair that lasts ten years is the greenest one.
Leo Brandt: Good. I'll do the rest of the deck. Can you get your page to me a week before it closes? I want time to put it together.
Robin Hale: A week before the deadline. Fine.
Leo Brandt: And prices: we use this year's list unless Nadia's new one is signed off in time.
