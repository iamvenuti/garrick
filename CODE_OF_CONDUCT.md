# Code of conduct

Garrick is for people who hold other people's confidences. Treat each other the way that work requires.

## Expected

- Be direct about the work and decent to the person.
- Assume the other person is competent and busy.
- Keep other people's material out of issues and pull requests: no real client names, notes or transcripts, yours or anyone else's.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults and personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Discriminatory language or behaviour of any kind.
- Posting someone's private information without their permission.
- Continuing after you've been asked to stop.

## Reporting

Report privately through **Report a vulnerability** on the [Security tab](https://github.com/iamvenuti/garrick/security/advisories/new) and start the title with "Conduct". It is the private channel this project has, and only the maintainer sees it. Reports are read within a week and kept confidential.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits and issues that break this code, and may block someone from the project, temporarily or for good. A first minor problem usually gets a private note; a serious one gets a ban without warning.

This code applies in the repository and anywhere someone represents the project. It is adapted from the ideas of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
