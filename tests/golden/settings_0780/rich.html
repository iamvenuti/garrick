How it keeps older material

Manage older material

Old meetings and reading change grade after the months set here: working pages are read by every answer, dormant ones only by a search that reaches back, archived ones only when you ask; nothing is deleted, and nothing moves until you say so.

A page older than its kind's months becomes dormant, and later archived. “What would fade” writes the full list; “fade the meetings” or “fade the library” moves exactly that list and keeps every link working. A page's age counts from the day its meeting took place (its date:), or the day its reading came out (its published:, else its date:). Without one, it counts from the date its file name starts with, such as 260314 or 2026-03-14. It never counts from when the page was last opened or changed. A date that names only a month or a year counts from the end of it, and a page with no date at all never fades by age. Saved when you close Settings.

Lets old pages of this wiki become dormant, then archived, so search stays on what matters. Turn it off to keep every page working. Nothing is deleted either way.

What keeps a page working

While any of these holds, a page stays working, however old. Person, organisation and topic pages, and the wiki's own lists, never move.

A page that a live thread links stays working, however old. Leave it on unless old pages stay working that you would rather let go.

A page that the notes of a live project link stays working, however old. Leave it on unless you want such pages to age.

A page that an open action in a Todo list links stays working, however old. Leave it on so a page you still need never goes dormant.

A page that says it is pinned stays working, however old. This is how you keep one page by hand.

Bringing a page back

When on, a dormant page that active work links again is proposed for coming back. Off, only a page marked as pinned comes back.

A dormant page is proposed to come back when a live thread, a live project's notes or an open action links it, where that protection is switched on, or when a working page links it afresh rather than through the link the move rewrote. Marking it pinned: true brings it back too, and is the only way back from the archive. Opening, reading or quoting a page brings nothing back, nothing comes back until you confirm a review, and a superseded page never comes back.
