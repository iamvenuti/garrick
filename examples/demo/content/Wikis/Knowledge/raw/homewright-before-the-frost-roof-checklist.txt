Before the Frost: A Roof Checklist
By the Homewright home team. Published 1 September 2026. Saved from the web, 11 September 2026.

A few slipped or cracked tiles rarely look urgent from the garden. The trouble starts when water gets under them and the first frost opens the gap.

Scaffolding is the cost people forget. On a small repair it is often half the bill, whoever does the work, so compare quotes on the repair itself.

Read your policy before you call anyone. Most home insurance treats slipped and worn tiles as wear and tear, which it does not cover. Damage from a storm usually is covered, but an insurer may refuse a claim if the roof was already in poor repair.

Ask for a written quote that prices the scaffolding separately from the repair, and get the work done before the first hard frosts.
