# Market Sizing

How big the Nordic market for Birch's mid-range pumps is, and what share Birch could realistically take. Produced the sizing memo for the board.

## The ask

Theo Marsh, at the [[Meetings/wiki/sources/260904-birch-kick-off|kick-off]]: "If the numbers don't hold up, I'd rather hear it in September than in March." Memo due 16 September.

## State of play

### Outcome

**Finished 17 September 2026.** Delivered `Deliverables/260916 - Market sizing memo.md`: about forty million euros a year addressable, three to four percent realistic by year two. Theo accepted it at the review and closed the work. The recommendation to the board on 28 September is entry through one distributor, not a subsidiary.

---

**17 September 2026.** Finished. The recommendation carries into the Launch Plan thread; the carrier question into Carrier Choice.

**16 September 2026.** Presented the memo to Theo and Iris ([[Meetings/wiki/sources/260916-birch-market-sizing-review|sizing review]]). Theo: "That's smaller than I hoped, but it's real." Decided, Theo's call: distributor-led entry, spring launch, carrier chosen before Christmas. "Close the sizing work. It's done."

**9 September 2026.** Sizing model built from public trade statistics and Iris's six distributor contacts. Three scenarios; the middle one carried into the memo.

**4 September 2026.** Thread opened at the kick-off.
