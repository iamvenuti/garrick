# Birch & Co: entering the Nordics

For the board on 28 September 2026. Sam Rivera, with Iris Bell. One page; the market sizing memo of 16 September is behind it.

## Recommendation

Enter the Nordic market for mid-range industrial pumps next year:

- through one distributor, chosen after the board from the two on the shortlist, Norrvale Industrial and Fjellgard Teknik, with no subsidiary in year one;
- with a launch in the spring;
- with a carrier chosen before Christmas, on two written quotes.

## Why

The market is about forty million euros a year, and a realistic share by year two is three to four percent. That is enough for a distributor, not for a subsidiary.
