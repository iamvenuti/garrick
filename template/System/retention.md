# Retention

What the numbers can't hold about which pages fade from working memory. The settings say how many months a kind of page stays working before it goes dormant, and how many more before the archive; this file is the judgment a person would add, read by the `nurture` skill each time it proposes a fade. A page this file says to keep stays where it is, and the proposal says why.

This file is Garrick's, as shipped, and an update replaces it. Read `System/retention.local.md` next, when it exists: it holds this workspace's own judgment, in plain words, and wins where the two differ. Change a rule there and nowhere else.

## Keep longer

- A meeting where something was decided outlives one that only exchanged updates.
- Keep anything that sets a price, a rate, a term or a date someone committed to, for as long as the work it governs is open.
- Keep the first meeting with a party, and the one where the relationship changed: a contract signed, a scope changed, a dispute raised.
- Keep a page that the only record of a commitment rests on, even when nothing links it.
- Keep reading that a live piece of work cites as its evidence, and the standards and contracts a project is held to.

## Let go sooner

- A meeting that only scheduled another meeting.
- A newsletter or an article whose news a later page already carries.
- A page that says what another page says, more briefly.

## Never

- Fading never deletes anything, and never edits a raw record. A page that should not exist is a different question, asked on its own.
- Fading never decides who may use a page. A page keeps its zone and its parties wherever it moves, and the walls hold in `dormant/` and `archive/` as they do in `wiki/`.
