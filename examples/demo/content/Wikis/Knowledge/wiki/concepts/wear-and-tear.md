# Wear and tear

Damage that comes from age and use rather than from an event. Home insurance generally does not pay for it, so the repair is the owner's cost.

## What sources say

- Homewright says most home policies treat slipped and worn tiles as wear and tear, and that a storm claim may be refused if the roof was already in poor repair. [[../sources/homewright-before-the-frost-roof-checklist|Before the Frost]]
