# Sizing model

Working note for the [[Birch Entry/Threads/Market Sizing/Market Sizing|Market Sizing]] thread. Built 9 September 2026; rerun 14 September at the price Lena asked for.

## Market

Mid-range pumps for light industry in the five Nordic countries: 41 million euros in 2025, flat in volume ([[Knowledge/wiki/sources/nfec-nordic-pump-market-review-2026|Nordic Pump Market Review 2026]]). Rounded to forty in the memo. Top down, from the published figures, cut to the segment Birch sells into ([[Knowledge/wiki/concepts/market-sizing|market sizing]]).

## Share by year two

| Scenario | Share | Market value |
|---|---|---|
| Low | 2% | 0.8 million euros |
| Middle, carried | 3.5% | 1.4 million euros |
| High | 5% | 2.0 million euros |

The range is what brands entering since 2020 reached in two years. Checked against the four distributors [[Meetings/wiki/people/iris-bell|Iris]] met in June that sell pumps ([[Meetings/wiki/sources/260907-birch-distributor-contacts|distributor contacts]]): two have a gap in the mid-range, which is where a new brand gets in.
