# The template hint

**The concept.** The template hint is one wall finding in place of ten, for a note whose repeated wording is held by many projects of one other walled zone and by many files of its own, which is what an old note template looks like.

**What it touches.** It sits beside the wall check, Nurture and the Checks card's wall question. The wall check does not change: no commit verdict moves, and the findings stand until the wording is common or gone. Only Nurture's grouping, its fix line for a quote and one more question on the card change.

**What was measured.** Counts only: four or more projects of the other zone, and four or more files of the note's own zone that hold every shared run. The test fixture is a note from ten copies of one template against ten projects of another zone, and one sentence lifted from one hub, which gets no hint.

**What would show it is wrong.** A lifted sentence that gets the hint, a hint that names a matched word or a project of the other zone, or a wall finding that disappears from the card while the wall check still reports it.
