# Seeing the Whole Chain

A vendor white paper from Brindle Systems, selling its control tower product, Brindle Beacon. Published in June 2026; the paper gives the month only, so the day in `published` is a stand-in.

- Brindle Systems says Beacon cuts late inbound shipments by 30 percent in the first year, and pays for itself within nine months. Brindle's own footnote says these results are self-reported by customers and not independently audited.
- Its practical advice holds up without the product: start with the parts that stop the line, map which come from a single supplier, and watch those first.
