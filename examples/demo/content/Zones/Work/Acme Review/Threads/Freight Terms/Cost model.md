## The commitment test

Last year Acme shipped fewer than 1,200 pallets in two quarters: 960 in the first and 1,060 in the fourth, 380 short in all. If shortfalls are charged at the full rate and counted by quarter, as is usual ([[Knowledge/wiki/sources/freight-ledger-shippers-push-back-on-volume-commitments|Shippers push back on volume commitments]]), that adds about 78,000 euros to the three percent. The commitment would then cost more than nine percent with no commitment at all.

What would change that:

- Counting the commitment over the year, not by quarter. Even then, 4,800 pallets a year needs growth of more than seven percent on last year.
- A lower commitment, nearer the slowest quarter ([[Knowledge/wiki/concepts/volume-commitments|volume commitments]]).
- Negotiating the fuel index and the commitment as one package ([[Knowledge/wiki/concepts/freight-rate-indexation|freight rate indexation]]).
