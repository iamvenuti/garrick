# Supply-chain visibility

Seeing orders, shipments and supplier commitments in one place, early enough to act before a line stops.

## What sources say

- Brindle Systems says its Beacon control tower cuts late inbound shipments by 30 percent; the figure is self-reported by its customers. Its advice to start with single-source parts that stop the line does not depend on the product. [[../sources/brindle-seeing-the-whole-chain|Seeing the Whole Chain]]
