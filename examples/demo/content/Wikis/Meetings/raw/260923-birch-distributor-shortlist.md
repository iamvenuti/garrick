# Birch & Co: distributor shortlist

Wednesday 23 September 2026, 10:00. Video call.
Present: Iris Bell, Ruth Kemp, Sam Rivera

**Iris Bell:** Ruth will run the distributor talks once the board says yes, so I've asked her in. We're down to two: Norrvale Industrial in Sweden and Fjellgard Teknik in Norway.

**Ruth Kemp:** Both carry pumps, and both have a gap in the mid-range. They want different things.

**Sam Rivera:** Such as?

**Ruth Kemp:** Norrvale wants exclusivity for all five countries. Fjellgard would take Norway and Sweden only, and leave us the rest.

**Iris Bell:** Five countries through one door is simpler.

**Ruth Kemp:** Simpler, until it goes wrong. I'd rather give exclusivity for two years than forever.

**Sam Rivera:** Then the agreement matters as much as the choice. I'll put together the questions it has to answer before you meet them.

**Ruth Kemp:** Please. I'd like them before my first calls, in the first week of October.

**Iris Bell:** And the board paper names both, with no choice yet. The board decides the entry, not the distributor.
