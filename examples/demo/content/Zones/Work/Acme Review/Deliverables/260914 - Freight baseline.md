# Acme Corp: freight baseline before the Cobalt Freight renewal

For Dana Whitlock. Sam Rivera, 14 September 2026.

## Last year with Cobalt Freight

| Quarter | Pallets |
|---|---|
| First | 960 |
| Second | 1,210 |
| Third | 1,240 |
| Fourth | 1,060 |
| Year | 4,470 |

Spend was 920,000 euros, about 206 euros a pallet. Eighty-five percent of the pallets went on the southern lanes. Fuel is a fixed surcharge of eleven percent on the base rate.

## What a rise would cost at these volumes

| Rise | A year |
|---|---|
| 1 percent | about 9,200 euros |
| 4 to 7 percent, the market range for renewals | about 37,000 to 64,000 euros |
| 9 percent | about 83,000 euros |

The market range is Pellow & Strand Research's, from its autumn outlook.

## For the call

Let Cobalt name its number first. Then ask what would move it: volume, term, or the fuel clause.
