# Case study: furnishing a university learning centre

A learning centre of 500 seats, furnished by Ashgrove in {last_year}.

The brief: chairs that survive students, desks with power at every seat, and furniture that can be rearranged by the cleaning staff in an evening.

What we delivered: 420 chairs, 120 desks and 30 tables, on time, with a ten-year warranty.

A year on: no frame failures, four mesh backs replaced under warranty.
