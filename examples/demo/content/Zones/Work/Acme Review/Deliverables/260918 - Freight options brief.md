# Acme Corp: freight options before the Cobalt Freight renewal

Draft for Dana Whitlock, Sam Rivera. First drafted 18 September 2026. Final due 2 October.

## The position

Acme's road freight contract with Cobalt Freight renews on 31 October. Cobalt has proposed nine percent. Independent research puts market renewals at four to seven percent for the same period, so nine is an opening position, not a price.

## Three options

| Option | Rate change | What Acme commits | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Renew with a volume commitment | +3% | 1,200 pallets a quarter, and a fuel index clause both ways | Paying for pallets Acme does not ship in a slow quarter |
| Renew without a commitment | +9% | Nothing new | The most expensive option, with nothing gained |
| Split the volume | to be quoted | Part of the volume to a second carrier | More to manage; a new carrier to qualify |

## Still to do

- Test the 1,200-pallet commitment against Dana's forecast by quarter. At last year's volumes Acme would have missed it in two quarters out of four.
- Cobalt's written offer, promised by 30 September.
- For the split option, a named second carrier. Fernway Logistics has announced new services and says it will price below the market; it has not been approached.

## Recommendation

Not yet. It depends on the forecast.
