# Corbel Supply Institute

An independent institute for purchasing and supply practice in manufacturing. It publishes surveys and practice guides.

## Claims

- From its 2025 survey of 210 manufacturers: a median of eleven weeks to qualify a second source for a machined, cast or moulded part. [[../sources/corbel-second-sources|Second Sources]]
