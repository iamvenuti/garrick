# Carriers race for Nordic capacity

A trade press article from The Freight Ledger on new road capacity into the Nordics.

- Cobalt Freight opened a cross-dock in southern Sweden in August. Cobalt says it will "add significant northbound capacity from next spring". Cobalt runs its northern service three times a week and gave no rates.
- Fernway Logistics says it will start a twice-weekly northern service in January and "price below the market to win the first customers". No figures given.
- The article's unnamed analysts expect the new space to ease pressure by mid-2027.
