# Nordic Fluid Equipment Council

A trade body for makers and importers of pumps, valves and compressors in the Nordic countries.

## Claims

- Its own figures: Nordic industrial pump sales of 1.12 billion euros in 2025, from its 46 members' reported sales and import statistics, not audited. [[../sources/nfec-nordic-pump-market-review-2026|Nordic Pump Market Review 2026]]
