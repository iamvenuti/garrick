# Using the Garrick App

The Garrick App shows where your work stands and what waits for you. It is not where the work happens. It opens a piece of work in the app you work in, and you carry on there. This page is the short version. The [full reference](extras/status-page.md) has every detail.

## The Overview

The Overview is the first tab. It opens on one sentence, such as "Three things need you" or "Nothing needs you right now". If it says nothing needs you, you can stop.

Below it are two cards. *Waiting on you now* lists what needs you, worst first, and each row has one button that fixes it. A late action, files not yet committed, a failed job and items in the inboxes all appear here. *Your work* lists every piece of work, the most recently touched first. Each row shows the next step, who it waits on and how it stands. A state word appears only when the work needs a look: *Late*, *Act now*, *Waiting* or *Look soon*. Work that is fine shows none. At the foot of the Overview, *Explore* draws a graph of your notes.

## Resume a piece of work

Find the row in *Your work* and press *Resume*. The arrow beside it lists other ways to open the same work. You choose the usual one in Settings, under *Opening work*.

*Claude* and *Codex* each open a new session in their own app, in the folder of that work, with a short request typed in. The request is not sent. Read it, then press Enter. Your assistant reads the work's resume point and tells you where you left off.

## What needs you

Anything that needs you shows in *Waiting on you now*. A row names the thing and gives one button. The Status tab is for the machinery behind the work, such as scheduled jobs and the state of each folder. It carries a number only when something needs attention.

The Context tab is for questions your notes raise. Each place that holds a question has one line with a count and a rough time, such as "6 questions, about 10 minutes". Press *Go through them* and answer one at a time. Each question has buttons, and nothing is changed until you press one. When there is nothing to answer, the card says so.

## Todo

The Todo tab lists every open action, one zone at a time. Pick the zone above the list. Tick the box to finish an action. Press *Add* above a zone's list to write a new one, with a date if you want. Late actions also appear on the Overview, so you see them without opening this tab.

Ticks, dates and new actions work in the Mac app. In a browser the page can only show them, and its buttons copy what to say to your assistant instead.

## Settings

The cog beside the page's name opens Settings. A list of its sections stays on the left. These are the ones you will use first.

- *Opening work* chooses which app a piece of work opens in, and which one is the usual choice.
- *Ask* sets how far the Ask box and a Telegram chat may go.
- *Notifications* chooses which things reach you, and when to stay quiet.
- *Walls* shows each zone and whether it is walled. What is in a walled zone stays in that zone.
- *Look* gives each zone a colour and an icon, and puts the page back as it was built.
- *About* shows your release and offers updates.

## When something looks wrong

The Guide tab holds these pages, so you can read them without leaving the App. A card that fails says what happened in plain words, and offers a button to try again or to see why. If a button is missing, you are probably in a browser. Open the Mac app and it appears.
