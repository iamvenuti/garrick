# Distribution Agreements in the Nordic Countries

A client briefing from Varne & Lisle, a law firm that advises exporters on distribution agreements in the Nordic countries. The briefing calls itself general information, not legal advice.

- Exporters entering the Nordics most often appoint one distributor, which buys, stocks and resells.
- Grant exclusivity country by country, tied to minimum purchase targets measured each year.
- Start with a fixed term of two or three years, renewable.
- Notice rules, and in some cases compensation on termination, differ between the countries: check each one before signing.
- Spell out resale pricing, within competition law, who pays for marketing, and what spares the distributor stocks.
