# Birch & Co: what we need from a carrier

For Iris Bell and Arno Vale, Sam Rivera. 18 September 2026. For every carrier we ask to quote.

| Need | Detail |
|---|---|
| Lane | Into the Nordics, door to door to the distributor's sites |
| Departures | Two a week at least; the distributor holds no more than ten days of stock |
| Delivery | Tail lift, since many sites have no loading dock |
| Claims | A named contact and a written claims process, with time limits |
| Volume | Forty to sixty pallets a month at first, to be confirmed |
| Start | The spring launch |
| Fuel | A fixed surcharge, or an index clause that names a public index and moves both ways |

Every carrier quotes against this page, so the quotes can sit side by side.
