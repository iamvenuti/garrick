# Distributor terms

Working note for the [[Birch Entry/Threads/Launch Plan/Launch Plan|Launch Plan]] thread: questions the distributor agreement has to answer, for [[Meetings/wiki/people/ruth-kemp|Ruth Kemp]]'s first calls. Drafted 26 September 2026. Not sent until the board has decided.

The two on the shortlist, from the [[Meetings/wiki/sources/260923-birch-distributor-shortlist|shortlist call]]: Norrvale Industrial wants all five countries, exclusively; Fjellgard Teknik would take Norway and Sweden. Ruth would rather grant exclusivity for two years than forever.

Questions, after Varne & Lisle's briefing ([[Knowledge/wiki/sources/varne-lisle-distribution-agreements-in-the-nordics|Distribution Agreements in the Nordic Countries]], [[Knowledge/wiki/concepts/distribution-agreements|distribution agreements]]):

1. Exclusivity: which countries, against what minimum purchases each year?
2. Term: two years fixed, then renewable?
3. Ending it: the notice, and whether compensation is owed, country by country. A lawyer checks this before anyone signs.
4. Prices: who sets resale prices, and within what limits.
5. Spares: what stock the distributor holds. Buyers want next-day spares more than a low price ([[Meetings/wiki/sources/260907-birch-distributor-contacts|distributor contacts]]).
6. Marketing: who pays for the launch.

The answers fill in section 1 of the [[Birch Entry/Threads/Launch Plan/Outline|outline]]. The board paper names both distributors and chooses neither ([[Meetings/wiki/sources/260925-birch-board-paper-review|board paper review]]).
