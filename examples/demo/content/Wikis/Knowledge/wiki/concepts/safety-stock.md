# Safety stock

Stock held beyond the usual reorder cycle, to cover a late delivery or a supplier failing. Bridge stock is safety stock held for a set time, such as while a second source is approved.

## What sources say

- The Corbel Supply Institute: size bridge stock to the qualification time of the second source, not to the usual reorder cycle. [[../sources/corbel-second-sources|Second Sources]]
