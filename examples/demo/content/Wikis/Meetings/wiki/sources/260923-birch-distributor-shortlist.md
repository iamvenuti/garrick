# Birch distributor shortlist

Video call, 23 September 2026. Sam Rivera with Iris Bell and Ruth Kemp.

## Decided

- The shortlist is Norrvale Industrial, in Sweden, and Fjellgard Teknik, in Norway. The board paper names both; the choice comes after the board.
- Ruth Kemp runs the distributor talks once the board has said yes.

## Wording that matters

- Ruth: "Norrvale wants exclusivity for all five countries. Fjellgard would take Norway and Sweden only, and leave us the rest."
- Ruth: "I'd rather give exclusivity for two years than forever."
- Iris: "The board decides the entry, not the distributor."
