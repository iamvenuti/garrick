## Seal kits first

Dana's call at the [[Meetings/wiki/sources/260924-acme-supplier-map-review|map review]]. What a second maker has to pass, from [[Meetings/wiki/people/felix-rook|Felix Rook]] at the [[Meetings/wiki/sources/260921-acme-seal-kit-qualification|qualification call]]: the drawings and spec, samples on the rig for eight weeks, then a first-article inspection. About ten weeks if nothing fails, so samples by mid-October for a second source by spring.

- Bridge stock from Keld, sized to the ten weeks rather than the usual reorder ([[Knowledge/wiki/concepts/safety-stock|safety stock]]). Seal kits harden on the shelf, so the bridge comes in two or three deliveries, not one.
- Once a second maker is approved, give it a real share, not a token order: Corbel's 80/20 ([[Knowledge/wiki/concepts/second-sourcing|second sourcing]], [[Knowledge/wiki/sources/corbel-second-sources|Second Sources]]).
- [[Meetings/wiki/people/owen-pike|Owen]]'s quote from the second maker is due 9 October. Compare unit price, lead time, minimum order, and how soon samples can come.
