# Freight Rate Outlook, Autumn 2026

An analyst note from Pellow & Strand Research, based on 140 contract renewals and interviews with eleven carriers.

- European road freight renewals due before the end of the first quarter of 2027 are expected to rise four to seven percent, driven mostly by wages and tolls.
- Carriers are swapping fixed fuel surcharges for index-linked clauses reviewed quarterly: about half of new contracts this year, against a quarter two years ago. The note advises accepting them only when they work both ways and name a public index.
- Northbound capacity into the Nordics is tight through the first quarter of 2027; new cross-dock space comes on line in the spring.
- Its advice: treat an increase above seven percent as an opening position, and trade volume for rate only against a forecast you would sign yourself.
