# Acme pump line walk-through

At Acme's plant, 8 September 2026. Sam Rivera with Hana Croft and Owen Pike.

## Decided

- Three parts can stop the pump line: seal kits, motor housings and control boards, each from a single supplier.
- The supplier map shows the stock cover for each of the three.

## Wording that matters

- Hana on stock: "Housings, about five weeks. Boards, four. Seal kits, two, sometimes less." Seal kits are kept low because "Rubber goes hard."
- Stoppages: "Twice in two years. Three days in March last year waiting on control boards, and a day in June when a seal-kit delivery went to the wrong site."
- Hana: "Seal kits are the one part I can't work around. No kit, no pump."
