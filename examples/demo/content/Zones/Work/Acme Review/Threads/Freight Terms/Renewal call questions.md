# Renewal call questions

Working note for the [[Acme Review/Threads/Freight Terms/Freight Terms|Freight Terms]] thread, for the call with Cobalt Freight on 15 September. Drafted 14 September 2026.

Going in with last year's [[Acme Review/Deliverables/260914 - Freight baseline|freight baseline]], built from [[Meetings/wiki/people/gil-thorne|Gil Thorne]]'s figures ([[Meetings/wiki/sources/260910-acme-freight-spend-baseline|freight spend baseline]]), and the "high single digits" Marta hinted at to Dana before the [[Meetings/wiki/sources/260902-acme-kick-off|kick-off]]. Pellow & Strand expect renewals of four to seven percent ([[Knowledge/wiki/sources/pellow-strand-freight-rate-outlook-autumn-2026|Freight Rate Outlook]]).

1. What is the number, and what is in it: wages, tolls, fuel?
2. What would bring it down: volume, a longer term, or the fuel clause?
3. If volume: how much, counted over what period, and what happens in a short quarter?
4. If a fuel index: which public index, reviewed how often, and does it move both ways ([[Knowledge/wiki/concepts/freight-rate-indexation|freight rate indexation]])?
5. When is the offer in writing?

Marta names the number first. [[Meetings/wiki/people/dana-whitlock|Dana]] leads the call; I ask the follow-ups.
