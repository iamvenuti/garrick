# Lane capacity

How much space carriers have on a given route, and so how much new volume a shipper can place there, and at what price.

## What sources say

- Northbound capacity into the Nordics is tight through the first quarter of 2027; new cross-dock space arrives in the spring. [[../sources/pellow-strand-freight-rate-outlook-autumn-2026|Freight Rate Outlook, Autumn 2026]]
- Cobalt Freight says its new Swedish cross-dock adds northbound capacity from spring 2027; Fernway Logistics says it starts a northern service in January. Analysts quoted by the article expect pressure to ease by mid-2027. [[../sources/freight-ledger-carriers-race-for-nordic-capacity|Carriers race for Nordic capacity]]
